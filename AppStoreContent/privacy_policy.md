# Privacy Policy for Simple Wire Creator

**Last Updated:** January 2026

## Overview

Simple Wire Creator is a Fusion 360 add-in that helps users create wire and cable geometry. We are committed to protecting your privacy and being transparent about our data practices.

## 1. Data Collection

**Simple Wire Creator does NOT collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data or information.**

Specifically, Simple Wire Creator:
- Does NOT collect any personal information
- Does NOT collect usage analytics or telemetry
- Does NOT access, store, or transmit any of your design data
- Does NOT communicate with any external servers
- Does NOT require an internet connection to function
- Does NOT use cookies or tracking mechanisms

## 2. Third-Party Data Sharing

Simple Wire Creator does NOT share data with any third parties because it does not collect any data. There are:
- No analytics tools
- No advertising networks
- No third-party SDKs that collect data
- No data sharing with affiliates or related entities

## 3. Data Retention and Deletion

Since Simple Wire Creator does not collect any user data, there is no data retention. No personal information is stored by the add-in at any time. The geometry you create is stored in your Fusion 360 design file, which is managed by Autodesk Fusion 360 according to their data policies.

## 4. Revoking Consent and Data Deletion Requests

Since Simple Wire Creator does not collect any personal data, there is no data to delete and no consent to revoke. If you wish to stop using the add-in, simply uninstall it from Fusion 360.

To uninstall:
1. Go to UTILITIES → Add-Ins → Scripts and Add-Ins
2. Find "Simple Wire Creator" in the Add-Ins list
3. Click the folder icon to open the installation directory
4. Close Fusion 360
5. Delete the SimpleWireCreator folder
6. Restart Fusion 360

## How Simple Wire Creator Works

Simple Wire Creator operates entirely within your local Fusion 360 environment. All operations are performed locally on your computer:
- Profile selections are processed in real-time
- Geometry calculations happen locally
- Created wire geometry is stored in your Fusion 360 design file
- No data leaves your computer

## Data Security

Since Simple Wire Creator does not collect any data, there is no data to secure. Your design files remain under your control and are subject to Autodesk's privacy policy for Fusion 360.

## Children's Privacy

Simple Wire Creator does not knowingly collect information from children under 13 years of age, as it does not collect any information at all.

## Changes to This Policy

If we make changes to this privacy policy, we will update the "Last Updated" date at the top of this document. Since Simple Wire Creator does not collect data, we do not anticipate needing to make significant changes.
