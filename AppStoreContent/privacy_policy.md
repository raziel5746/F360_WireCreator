# Privacy Policy for Wire Creator

**Last Updated:** January 2026

## Overview

Wire Creator is a Fusion 360 add-in that helps users create wire and cable geometry. We are committed to protecting your privacy and being transparent about our data practices.

## Data Collection

**Wire Creator does NOT collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data or information.**

Specifically, Wire Creator:
- Does NOT collect any personal information
- Does NOT collect usage analytics or telemetry
- Does NOT access, store, or transmit any of your design data
- Does NOT communicate with any external servers
- Does NOT require an internet connection to function
- Does NOT use cookies or tracking mechanisms

## How Wire Creator Works

Wire Creator operates entirely within your local Fusion 360 environment. All operations are performed locally on your computer:
- Profile selections are processed in real-time
- Geometry calculations happen locally
- Created wire geometry is stored in your Fusion 360 design file
- No data leaves your computer

## Third-Party Services

Wire Creator does NOT integrate with or share data with any third-party services.

## Data Security

Since Wire Creator does not collect any data, there is no data to secure. Your design files remain under your control and are subject to Autodesk's privacy policy for Fusion 360.

## Children's Privacy

Wire Creator does not knowingly collect information from children under 13 years of age, as it does not collect any information at all.

## Changes to This Policy

If we make changes to this privacy policy, we will update the "Last Updated" date at the top of this document. Since Wire Creator does not collect data, we do not anticipate needing to make significant changes.
